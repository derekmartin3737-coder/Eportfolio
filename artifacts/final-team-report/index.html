Autonomous Piano Player

Musical Bot Encore · Team 044 · 2025–2026 capstone. A modular robot designed to play a household keyboard through electronically controlled solenoids, making the connection between software and physical motion visible to an outreach audience.

Open Final Drawing Package PDF Open Design Changes PDF Open Final BOM

Engineering problem

The team needed to turn digital note events into repeatable key presses while keeping the robot portable, adjustable, and non-invasive to the keyboard. Timing, key alignment, switching speed, and power distribution shaped the design.

My contribution

I developed Python control software and supported CAD, mechanical integration, and system testing. The linked drawings, bill of materials, and change record are shared team deliverables.

Design decisions

The proposal favored a fixed solenoid array to support overlapping notes. It identified relay switching speed as a limitation and proposed MOSFET switching. The final design record documents a T-slotted 6063 aluminum frame, separate white- and black-key assemblies, and custom 3D-printed solenoid mounting plates.

The modular layout supported adjustment and subsystem development, with the tradeoff of additional wiring, driver channels, and power-distribution work.

Documented outcome

The final package contains assembly and part drawings, electronics mounts, a bill of materials, and a record of design changes. The proposal describes performance targets; these documents do not provide final measured timing, reliability, or setup-time results.
