Winch Lift Stand

An aluminum support stand for a winch lifting a 150 lb motor approximately 50 ft.

Engineering problem

Support the winch and lifting load with an aluminum stand. The approximately 50 ft dimension describes the lift travel, not the stand height.

My contribution

I designed and analyzed the stand, applying mechanical design and structural analysis to evaluate the support under the intended load.

Design and analysis

The project focused on evaluating the load-bearing structure and factor of safety for the intended lifting application.

Outcome

Completed the aluminum stand design and structural analysis for the intended motor-lifting task.
