Evensol internship · June–September 2024

Fan-Motor Hand-Crank System

A mechanical maintenance mechanism developed alongside field work at a landfill-gas facility.

Engineering problem

Develop a hand-crank system for fan-motor maintenance and examine how the assembly carries its loads.

My contribution

I designed and modeled the system in Autodesk Fusion 360, performed stress analysis to evaluate loading and potential weak points, and fabricated and fitted steel components for the assembly.

Methods and deliverables

The work connected CAD modeling and simulation with physical fit-up and fabrication. My deliverables included the model, analysis work, and fitted steel components.
